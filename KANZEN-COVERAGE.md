# Shin Kanzen Master N2: coverage audit

Reviewed: 11 October 2026. ISBN 9784883195657.

**Supplied checklist mapped; full printed-index verification pending.**

The [publisher](https://www.3anet.co.jp/np/books/3602/) advertises 211 grammar forms. Its public contents do not provide the complete form index or every teaching point. This report verifies links from the supplied list and coverage of public topic headings; it does not certify 211/211 forms, all explanations, every vocabulary gloss, or the book's mock exams. The app uses original explanations and examples.

## What is covered

- 156 supplied checklist rows, linked to 171 distinct lessons (some rows contain several forms; some repeat a sense).
- A short English and Thai key clue on each of those 171 lessons.
- 22 additional support lessons for the later published topic headings: seven organization, three sentence-building and twelve passage skills. These are introductions, not exhaustive transcriptions of those sections.
- 28 original practice questions for those skills, including all three JLPT grammar formats.
- 36 mapped lessons labelled N3 remain included when studying N2. Existing IDs and progress are preserved.
- Specific corrections: both verb attachments for 上で, negative verbs with ところだった, 得る readings, and visible aliases for perspective にしても, というわけではない, おかげだ and せいだ.

## What remains unverified

To certify every grammar form, compare the complete printed 索引 (index) against this mapping. To certify every useful book explanation or keyword, the relevant section pages must also be available for review. Neither a lesson total of 467 nor the 156 checklist rows proves the publisher's 211-form coverage. Repeated forms, aliases and split senses prevent a valid percentage calculation.

The app does not reproduce the book's vocabulary glossary, chapter exercises or two mock tests. Its random drill remains a separately sourced and authored practice bank.
